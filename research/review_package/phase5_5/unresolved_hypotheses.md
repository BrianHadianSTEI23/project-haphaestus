# Phase 5.5 — Unresolved Hypotheses and Open Questions

1. **Separated-Target / Anti-Forward Discrimination (`H_sep`):**
   - Because target and distractor waypoints in `PAIR_000`–`PAIR_059` lie at mean $x \approx 0.66\text{ m}$ and are separated laterally by only **$0.317\text{ m} < 0.35\text{ m}$** reach radius, forward driving suffices for ~75% target reach even when target pixels are masked (`C3`). Future evaluation on wider angular separations ($> 0.70\text{ m}$ lateral separation or wider left/right camera angles) is needed to quantify pure task-selective closed-loop discrimination free of forward-reach overlap.
2. **Task-Token Swapping Paradox & `C5` vs `C1` Non-Difference (`H_swap_null`):**
   - `C5_swapped_task_token` achieved `52/60 (86.7%)` vs `C1_full_closed_loop` `51/60 (85.0%)` (McNemar $p=1.0$, Holm $p=1.0$), with `r(gv0_y, gt_y)` flipping from `-0.288` (`C1`) to `+0.261` (`C5`). We do NOT claim `C5` and `C1` are equivalent; rather, in this checkpoint and dual-visible close-pair geometry, swapped tokens did not induce closed-loop failure (`redir = 2/60` vs `1/60`). Why `grounding_head` exhibits an inverted baseline task sensitivity in static step-0 (`eta_gt = -0.5516`) remains unexplained at training-data / loss-function level.
3. **Pixel-Masking (`C3` vs `C4`) Non-Significance & Diffuse Policy Bypass (`H_bypass`):**
   - `C3_target_masked_rgb` (`75.0%`) vs `C4_distractor_masked_rgb` (`76.7%`) showed McNemar $p=1.0$ (Holm $p=1.0$, non-significant — equivalence NOT established). Although trajectory inspection shows `C3` steered toward the unmasked distractor (`r(lat, gd_y) = +0.477`) and ended closer to the distractor more often (`39/60`), why removing target pixels in `C3` still left `g_visual` pointing broadly forward (`|gv| = 1.168`) and sustaining `fwd = 0.440 m` indicates scene-level/floor-frame forward priors in `grounding_head` and `policy_head(s_t, goal_t)`.
4. **Occultation / Out-of-View Memory Role (`H_mem_occluded`):**
   - `A1_visual_only_no_memory` (`83.3%`) vs `C1` (`85.0%`, McNemar $p=1.0$) shows negligible goal-memory dependence in continuously visible dual-target scenes. Whether ego-motion goal memory (`0.35 * mem_updated`) provides causal rescue under transient occlusion or target disappearance after $t=0$ remains untested in Phase 5.5.
5. **Distributed / Diffuse Circuit Coding Beyond Layer 1/Layer 2 Sparse Subsets (`H_diffuse`):**
   - Consistent with Phase 5.4.2, distributed or cross-layer causal task selectivity remains unestablished and cannot be promoted from null/incomplete-support interventions.
