# Phase 5.6 Unresolved Hypotheses & Scientific Boundaries

1. **Nonlinear Decodability in `z_t` (Beyond E1 Linear Probes):** Stage B4 established that a linear Ridge probe cannot decode category-bound object identity/position (`obj_1_xy`, `assigned_wp_xy`, `rel_dir_xy`: `R2 <= +0.01`) from `z_t (32D)`, and captures only `~3.4 mm` (`dMAE`) of slot-wise `left_obj_xy` / `right_obj_xy` variance. Whether a small nonlinear MLP probe could extract higher-variance geometric detail from `z_t` remains open, though Stage B5 (`F2_donor_flipped_lr`) already proves `grounding_head` itself does not transform `z_t` into a left/right-symmetric target-bound waypoint (`96.7%` right predictions even when assigned object is on Left).
2. **Closed-Loop Emergence of Apparent Rightward Reaches:** In Phase 5.5.1.1, dynamic visual feedback (`F1`) achieved `32/120` assigned reaches (`27/60` Right vs `5/60` Left), whereas freezing initial predictions (`F2`) reduced assigned success. Phase 5.6 proves `g_vis` starts with a strong rightward offset (`85.3%` `dy_b < 0`, `mean -0.351 m`) driven by `"approach"` token-category offsets and `75.9%` rightward training labels. Why repeated closed-loop visual updates (`F1`) slightly aid reaching when turning right (potentially via optical-flow / obstacle centering or proximity shrinkage near the camera once turning right brings a right object dead-ahead) was not dynamically intervened on beyond fixed step-0 images and trajectory replays.
3. **Training Data / Architecture Remedy (Out of Scope Without Retraining):** Because `hjepa_best.pt` was frozen (`E2` shows zero causal task-to-object binding circuit in the trained checkpoint), testing whether left-right balanced `approach` training episodes, higher scene-layout diversity (`>96` layouts), or spatial-attention / bilinear object-key fusion architectures create a genuine causal binding circuit requires future controlled retraining experiments.
