## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This package contains the complete experimental data, causal interventions, statistical tests, forensic corrections, and raw artifact traces from the **Phase 5.1 Experimental Validity Correction and Mechanistic Revalidation** of Project Hephaestus.

In accordance with the scientific integrity protocol:
1. **No Speculative Narratives:** We do not manufacture a unified intelligence theory or prematurely promote unresolved hypotheses into principles.
2. **Preservation of Negative Results:** The dual target discrimination rate under paired counterfactual scenes ($16.0\%$) is statistically indistinguishable from chance ($19.5\%$, one-sided $p=0.9890$). Isolated modular subcircuits are falsified (candidate units do not beat random dimension-matched subsets after Holm-Bonferroni correction, $p > 0.05$). These negative results are preserved.
3. **Invalidation of Methodologically Flawed Claims:** The historical 5.0% "same-category instance binding" result is formally **INVALIDATED** because the task vocabulary (`encode_task_id()`) has zero capacity to index identical object instances, and the underlying simulator initialization failed to inject custom obstacles.
4. **Physical Separability:** Translational errors (meters) and angular errors (radians/degrees) are strictly decoupled and never pooled into an unnormalized Euclidean distance.
5. **Exact Replay & Provenance:** All 1,500 per-step records for all 50 blackout trajectories are saved in line-delimited JSONL format with complete state transitions, actions, physical displacements, and waypoint tracking errors.

## 3. Key Quantitative Findings & Statistical Evidence

### 3.1 Task-Conditioned Binding & Spatial Decoding
- **Sample:** 50 independent pairs (100 scenes, 200 forward evaluations).
- **Discrimination Performance:**
  - Marginal accuracy: $46.5\%$ ($93/200$, 95% CI: $[41.0\%, 52.0\%]$).
  - Dual discrimination rate: $16.0\%$ ($16/100$ scenes, 95% CI: $[9.0\%, 24.0\%]$).
  - Both scenes dual success in a pair: **0/50 (0.0%)**.
- **Pair-Clustered Permutation Null (100,000 resamples):**
  - Null Mean $= 19.5\%$ (95% CI: $[16.0\%, 23.0\%]$).
  - $p_{\text{one\_sided}} = 0.9890$, $p_{\text{two\_sided}} = 0.0571$.
  - **Conclusion:** Dual discrimination is statistically indistinguishable from chance.
- **Factorial Interaction Vector ($I = (g_{bb} - g_{ba}) - (g_{ab} - g_{aa})$):**
  - Mean norm: $\|I\| = 0.3521$ m (95% CI: $[0.2921, 0.4161]$ m).
  - Cosine alignment with physical target swap $\Delta P$: $\overline{\cos} = 0.3140$ (95% CI: $[0.1508, 0.4693]$).
  - Angular divergence: $66.9^\circ$ (95% CI: $[55.3^\circ, 78.9^\circ]$).
  - Sign-flip tests (100,000 resamples): $p_{\text{pos}} = 0.0002$, $p_{\text{neg}} = 0.9999$, $p_{\text{two}} = 0.0004$.
  - **Conclusion:** Falsifies both clean dynamic binding (collinear alignment) and additive bias ($I=0$). Confirms **Mechanism 3: Mixed Nonlinear Interaction**.
- **Stratified Grouped 5-Fold Cross-Validation (Partitioned by Pair Identity):**
  - Comparison 1 (H-JEPA latent $z$): Bilinear MSE ($0.00544$) and Nonlinear MLP MSE ($0.00439$) decisively outperform Additive ($0.01985$) and Task-Only ($0.03723$).
  - Comparison 2 (Ground-Truth targets): Bilinear MSE ($0.00274$) and Nonlinear MLP MSE ($0.00367$) decisively outperform Additive ($0.01249$) and Visual Baseline ($0.01276$).
- **Instance-Level Binding:**
  - Audited `encode_task_id()`. The vocabulary contains 6 color landmarks $\times$ 8 relations. There are zero slots or relational indices to represent visually identical instances.
  - The historical 5.0% claim is **INVALIDATED** and classified as `ARCHITECTURALLY_UNSUPPORTED`.
